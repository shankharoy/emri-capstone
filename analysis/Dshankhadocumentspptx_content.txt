The Market Entry Timing Problem
Current Challenge
FMCG firms deploy hundreds of billions annually in emerging markets

Traditional approaches rely on GDP per capita — a lagging indicator that:
Reflects historical performance, not future readiness
Oversights structural prerequisites for digital commerce
Leads to premature or delayed market entry
Consequences
Capital Misallocation
Investments in markets lacking digital infrastructure or urban density
Missed Opportunities
Late entry into high-growth markets after competitors establish dominance
Suboptimal ROI
3–5-year planning horizons undermined by structural misalignment
